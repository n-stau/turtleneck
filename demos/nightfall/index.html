Questions, answered

How does the free trial work?

You get 14 days of Nightfall+ with every title unlocked. Add a payment method to start. We remind you 3 days before the trial ends, and you can cancel in two taps before then without paying.

What can I watch?

Thrillers, mystery series and documentaries only. About 1,200 titles at launch, with new originals every month. No sports, no reality TV.

Can I change or cancel my plan?

Yes. Switch plans or cancel in Account at any time. Changes apply at the next billing date, and you keep access until then.

How many devices can I use?

Sign in on as many devices as you like. The plan sets how many can stream at once: 1 on Essential, 2 on Standard, 4 on Premium.

Can I download titles to watch offline?

Standard and Premium can download most titles on phones and tablets. Downloads stay for 30 days, or 48 hours once you press play.

Is there a kids mode?

Each profile can be set to Kids. Kids profiles hide thrillers and mysteries rated above 12 and have no sign-in to other profiles.
