Questions before you start

Can we import from spreadsheets or another CRM?

Yes. Upload CSV files or connect your old system, map the fields once and review the result before anything goes live.

How is a user counted?

Anyone who signs in with edit rights. Viewers on Business and Scale are free, up to 5 per paid user.

Where is our data stored?

In the US by default. Scale plans can choose the EU region, and all data is encrypted at rest and in transit.

Can we leave and take our data?

Export everything as CSV or through the API at any time, on every plan.
